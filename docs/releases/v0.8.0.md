For agencies and anyone who reports the numbers to someone else.

## What's new
- **All sites (`/sites`)**: every GA4 property the signed-in account can read, each with the board's summary sentence and three tiles. Properties other than the open one get a first guess of visitor behavior (marked 仮). The list and the open site are free; the other sites' summaries need Pro (`billing` `site_compare`). "Open board" switches the board to that property.
- **Print / PDF** in the board's top bar: print CSS without the menu, memo panel, table of contents and controls, with a one-line header (site · period · date).

## Better
- "What to look at now" tips by visitor behavior (`i18n/watch_tips_l10n.py`), falling back to the metric's common tip.
- Cited industry targets show the date their figures were last checked.
- A "Not tracked" step that GA4 enhanced measurement records (scroll, outbound click, site search, video, file download, forms) points to the switch in GA4.
- 詳しい数字 GA4 batch cached 5 min per property · period (period switches no longer re-run it).

## Under the hood
- `story_reach` event (how far down the board people read); HQ API adds `story` and `feedback` summaries.
- `.dockerignore` excludes `x-key`.
- Docs: `docs/launch/` (OAuth review, SiteKPI's own GA4 setup, benchmark source terms), `docs/design/weekly-mail.md`.
