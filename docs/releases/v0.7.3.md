Answers "which page should I fix?".

## What's new
- **Who is converting? → Which landing pages lead to the goal**: visitors and conversion rate per landing page, with the same split as the traffic map (grow · core · fix · watch).
- **Where is it happening? → On which page?**: the problem step's rate per landing page, on the same ruler as devices and traffic; the lowest page is outlined and named in *What to do*.
- **Detailed numbers → By page**: most viewed pages with views, visits started there, engagement rate and conversions. Sortable, 10 rows then *Show more* (up to 30); cards on phones.
- Page names link to the page on your own site (new tab). The domain comes from GA4's host name; no link for localhost or sample boards.

## Under the hood
- Three more GA4 reports in the same batch (landing page, landing page × event, page path × title × host). No new Google permission.
- Paths are merged without `?…` and a trailing `/`; the site-name tail is dropped from page titles.
