The board reads as a story instead of a list of numbers.

## What's new
- **Seven chapters**: what's going well · why did it change? · who is converting? · where is it happening? · since when? · what to do · summary.
- **Who is converting?** A traffic map (volume vs. conversion rate) and new vs. returning visitors.
- **Since when?** An 8-week chart with the industry band, so you can see when a step started to slip.
- **Did the fix work?** Record the day you shipped a change; 14 days later the board compares the 14 days before and after.
- Japanese, English and Korean.

## Fixes in this release
- Story dates use a fixed +09:00 offset (slim images have no time-zone database) — #33
- Live tests fake the story's GA4 reports — #34
- Natural Korean wording for chapter 1 — #35
