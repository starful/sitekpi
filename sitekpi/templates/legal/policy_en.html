2. Scope

This policy applies to every page on sitekpi.net (home, board, metric glossary, sample boards, settings and so on) and to the information the Service obtains through Google APIs. External sites linked from the Service (Google, the research firms cited as sources, etc.) are covered by their own policies.

3. Information we collect

The Service has no sign-up, and we never ask for your name, email address, phone number or postal address. We collect only the following.

  • Google credentials: the OAuth access and refresh tokens issued when you press “Connect GA4” and approve on Google's screen. The Service never receives your Google account password.
  • GA4 lists and aggregated reports: the names and IDs of the GA4 accounts and properties you can see, and aggregated figures for the one property you choose (totals and rates of users, sessions, page views, events, traffic sources, devices, revenue and so on). We do not request data that identifies individual visitors.
  • Board settings: the property, industry, period and display language you chose, the flow steps and event names you fixed, side paths, and your own target ranges.
  • Feedback: the note or comment (up to 500 characters), the optional 👍/👎, the screen, the part of the screen you were looking at and the path, display language, connection state (connected / not connected) and time of anything sent from “Notes & feedback” or “Feedback”. Notes you sent are also listed in your own browser (local storage) and are not sent again.
  • How the Service is used: pages opened, buttons pressed, display language and connection state, measured with Google Analytics (see section 6).
  • Access logs: the IP address, browser type (User-Agent), URL, time and response code that the server records automatically when a page is opened, using Google Cloud's standard logging.

4. Purposes of use

We use the information only for the following purposes.

  1. Showing the board, visitor flow and metrics of the GA4 property you chose, compared with the previous period and industry benchmarks
  2. Showing the board with the same settings next time
  3. Detecting and preventing unauthorised access or excessive load, and investigating failures
  4. Understanding how the Service is used (which screens and features)
  5. Improving screens, wording and features based on your feedback
  6. Answering enquiries

Before using information for any other purpose, we will update this policy and ask for consent where required.

5. Google user data

  • Scope: we request only https://www.googleapis.com/auth/analytics.readonly. We never request permission to change GA4 settings or data.
  • Use: only to display the screens you open. To make pages faster, report results are kept in server memory for up to 5 minutes. Report results are never written to a database.
  • Human access: no person, including the operator, reads your GA4 data, except with your explicit consent, when needed for a security investigation, or to comply with the law.
  • What we never do: sell Google user data; use it for advertising (including targeted, retargeting or interest-based ads); use it to determine creditworthiness or for lending; transfer it to data brokers; or use it to develop or train general-purpose AI or machine-learning models.

SiteKPI’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Cookies and data sent to third parties

The Service uses two kinds of cookies. We do not use advertising cookies or social media buttons.

  • Session cookie: a random session ID that keeps you signed in. It contains no tokens and no GA4 data.
  • Google Analytics cookies (such as _ga): used to learn how the Service itself is used (pages opened, buttons pressed, display language, connection state) and to improve the screens. We never send the name, ID or numbers of your GA4 property, or anything else received from Google APIs. To opt out, use the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

As required by Japan's Telecommunications Business Act, we disclose the information your browser sends to outside services.

Feedback may be used, aggregated so that no individual can be identified, in reports on improvements.

  • Country: United States of America

10. Security measures

  • All traffic is encrypted with HTTPS.
  • OAuth tokens are encrypted on the server (Fernet), with the key kept in Secret Manager.
  • Only the operator's accounts can access the database and servers, and they use two-step verification.
  • Dependencies are updated regularly to address known vulnerabilities.
  • If a data breach occurs, we will report it to the Personal Information Protection Commission and notify users as required by law.

11. Deleting data and disconnecting

  • Disconnect from “Connect / disconnect” in the menu to delete your session and revoke the token at Google.
  • You can also remove SiteKPI's access at any time from Third-party apps with account access in your Google account.
  • To delete board settings saved for a property or feedback you sent, contact us (section 15). We will confirm the property ID and delete them within 30 days.
