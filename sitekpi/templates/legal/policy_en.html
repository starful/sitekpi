Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

This policy explains what SiteKPI (sitekpi.net, “the service”) collects, how it is used, and how you can remove it.

1. What the service does

SiteKPI shows the Google Analytics 4 (GA4) numbers of a website you choose and compares them with the previous period and with published industry benchmarks. Access to GA4 is read-only.

2. Google user data we access

  • Scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/analytics.readonly only.
  • What we read: the list of GA4 accounts and properties you can see, and aggregated reports for the one property you select (users, sessions, events, channels, devices, revenue and similar totals). We do not read data about individual visitors.
  • How we use it: only to draw your board, the flow and the metric pages you open. Report results are kept in server memory for up to 5 minutes to make the page faster, and are not written to a database.
  • What we never do: we do not change your GA4 settings or data, sell or share Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI or machine-learning models.

SiteKPI’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. What we store

  • Sign-in session: the Google OAuth tokens and the property you selected are kept in a server-side session, encrypted at rest. Your browser only holds a random session ID in a cookie. Sessions expire after 14 days without use.
  • Board settings per GA4 property: the flow steps and event names you edit and your custom benchmark ranges, so the same property shows the same settings next time.
  • Feedback you send: the screen, your 👍/👎 and the comment you write, the display language, and whether a property was connected. We do not store your IP address or Google account with feedback.

Data is stored on Google Cloud (Cloud Run and Firestore).

Measuring how SiteKPI itself is used: to improve the screens, we use Google Analytics 4 through Google Tag Manager to record pages opened, buttons pressed, language and connection state (demo / connected / not connected), with cookies such as _ga. We never send the name, ID or numbers of your GA4 property, or anything else received from Google APIs. We do not use advertising cookies. To opt out, use the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

4. Removing your data

  • Disconnect (menu → Connection) deletes your session and revokes the token at Google.
  • You can also remove SiteKPI’s access at any time from your Google Account: Third-party apps with account access.
  • To delete the board settings saved for a property, contact us (section 6).

5. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.
